                    DRONE HUNTER 2D: ULTIMATE SCI-FI ARCADE EDITION

Welcome to Drone Hunter 2D, a high-octane tactical drone flight-shooter featuring 
procedural synthwave audio, environmental hazards, dynamic 4-phase escalation waves, 
4 specialized boss drone encounters, and 15 progressive campaign stages!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FLIGHT CONTROLS & TACTICAL KEYBINDINGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  * PILOT FLIGHT & STEERING :  W, A, S, D  or  ARROW KEYS
  * RETICLE AIM & TARGETING :  MOUSE POINTER
  * PRIMARY CANNON FIRE     :  LEFT MOUSE BUTTON (Hold for continuous fire)
  * EMP SHOCKWAVE BLAST     :  RIGHT MOUSE BUTTON  or  [ E ] KEY
  * CYCLE WEAPON LOADOUT    :  [ TAB ] KEY (Pulse / Scatter / Missile / Laser)
  * EVASIVE BARREL ROLL     :  [ LEFT SHIFT ] KEY (Invulnerable dodging roll)
  * TACTICAL CLOAKING UNIT  :  [ C ] KEY  or  [ K ] KEY (Invisibility stealth)
  * PAUSE & SETTINGS PANEL  :  [ P ] KEY  or  [ ESC ] KEY
  * CRT SCANLINES FILTER    :  [ F2 ] KEY
  * QUICK EXIT GAME         :  [ Q ] KEY

2. CAMPAIGN SECTORS & 4 UNIQUE BOSS DRONES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The campaign spans 5 unique environment sectors, featuring 3 progressive 
sub-level stages per sector:

  [SECTOR 1] MEGACITY SKYLINE (Neon Rooftops & Airborne Chasers)
    * Stage 1-1: Recon Patrol (Goal: 1,800 PTS)
    * Stage 1-2: Laser Grid Assault (Goal: 3,500 PTS)
    * Stage 1-3: Sky Dreadnought Boss ☠️ (Goal: 6,000 PTS)
      Boss Power: 360-Degree Radial Spiral Bullet Salvos.

  [SECTOR 2] CYBER FACTORY CORE (Industrial Foundry & Molten Hazards)
    * Stage 2-1: Foundry Gates (Goal: 3,000 PTS)
    * Stage 2-2: Furnace Breach (Goal: 6,000 PTS)
    * Stage 2-3: EMP Disrupter Boss ☠️ (Goal: 10,000 PTS)
      Boss Power: Expanding EMP Shockwave that jams player weapons for 1.5s!

  [SECTOR 3] ORBITAL SPACE CITADEL (Meteor Storms & Gravity Anomalies)
    * Stage 3-1: Asteroid Field (Goal: 5,000 PTS)
    * Stage 3-2: Citadel Defense Grid (Goal: 9,500 PTS)
    * Stage 3-3: Stealth Mirage Boss ☠️ (Goal: 15,000 PTS)
      Boss Power: Invisibility Cloak & Holographic Decoy Clones.

  [SECTOR 4] STORMY OCEAN BATTLESCAPE (Ocean Tempests & Spiked Sea Mines)
    * Stage 4-1: Tempest Swells (Goal: 7,500 PTS)
    * Stage 4-2: Naval Fleet Siege (Goal: 14,000 PTS)
    * Stage 4-3: Leviathan EMP Warship ☠️ (Goal: 22,000 PTS)

  [SECTOR 5] NEON SUN DESERT WASTELAND (Cyber Pyramids & Sandstorm Turrets)
    * Stage 5-1: Dune Canyons (Goal: 10,000 PTS)
    * Stage 5-2: Monolith Ruins (Goal: 18,000 PTS)
    * Stage 5-3: Colossus Titan Mech Boss ☠️ (Goal: 30,000 PTS)
      Boss Power: 3-Phase Encounter with Revolving Orbital Shields & Overclock Berserk Mode!

3. WEAPONS BAY & ARMORY LOADOUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. PULSE CANNON      : High-rate dual energy bolts. (Unlocked default)
  2. SCATTER SHOTGUN   : 5-bolt widespread salvo for crowd control.
  3. HOMING MISSILES   : Self-guided tracking rockets for heavy targets.
  4. HIGH-LEVEL LASER  : Electric cyan-white thermal plasma beam with high damage output.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. DRONE HANGAR SHOP UPGRADES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Earn Gold Scrap ($) by destroying drones and bosses to upgrade your Quadcopter:
  * Max Battery Capacity      : Increases maximum health (HP).
  * Thruster Agility          : Enhances horizontal flight movement speed.
  * Cannon Fire-Rate          : Accelerates primary weapon firing rate.
  * EMP Shockwave Charger     : Speeds up EMP recharge rate.
  * Wingman Support Drones    : Deploys up to 2 autonomous escort minidrones.
  * Tactical Cloaking Unit    : Unlocks 4-second stealth cloak ability.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. DIFFICULTY MODES & PAUSE SETTINGS MENU
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Press [P] or [ESC] during play to access the interactive Settings Menu:
  * EASY 🟢     : Reduced enemy damage (-40%) & slower enemy bullets.
  * NORMAL 🔵   : Balanced standard arcade experience.
  * HARD ⚠️     : Fast salvos (+30% speed) & high target health.
  * NIGHTMARE ☠️: Extreme bullet-hell difficulty & fast boss rage salvos!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. GRAND VICTORY & ENDLESS NIGHTMARE MODE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Clearing Stage 5-3 (Desert Colossus Titan Mech) unlocks the Grand Campaign 
Victory Champion Screen and unlocks Endless Nightmare Survival Mode for 
unlimited high-score challenges!
