================================================================================
                    DRONE HUNTER 2D - QUICK START & README
================================================================================

HOW TO PLAY:
1. Double-click `DroneHunter.exe` or run `python main.py` in CMD.
2. Select your Sector Stage (Stage 1-1 to Stage 5-3) from the Sector Campaign Map.
3. Use W, A, S, D to fly, MOUSE to aim, and LEFT CLICK to fire weapons!
4. Press [P] or [ESC] at any time to open the Pause & Settings panel to change 
   Difficulty, toggle CRT Scanlines, or mute/unmute Audio SFX.

Full detailed guide available in `GAME_GUIDE.txt`.
================================================================================
